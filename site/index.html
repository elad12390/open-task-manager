Open Task Manager

A fast, native, open-source system monitor for macOS. See every process as a tree, what each app really costs, and act on it — all locally.

brew install --cask elad12390/tap/open-task-manager

Recorded in the app's demo mode, which shows a fictional Mac.

Everything you expect from a task manager — and more

⌥

A real process tree

launchd at the root, the operating system's processes and yours in separate branches, grouped by subsystem and by the app that owns them.

Σ

Memory per app

Every branch sums its whole subtree, so a browser and all its helpers show up as one number.

⌕

Dynamic filters

Autocomplete chips for apps, users, categories and states, plus cpu>5 and mem>1g thresholds.

⌘

Right-click actions

Quit, force quit (with children), suspend and resume, show in Finder, show connections, copy details. macOS's own processes are protected.

⇅

Keyboard first

↑ ↓ to move, ← → to fold, Space to toggle, ⌥→ to open a whole branch. Folding folds everything inside.

▦

Your columns

Show, hide, drag to reorder, drag to resize, sort, and scroll sideways. App icons keep it scannable.

◎

The whole system

Overview, per-core load, network, disk space with scan history, power and thermal state, users and open connections.

⏻

Manage startup and services

Enable or disable your launch agents, restart or stop services, uninstall apps to the Trash.

●

Record and benchmark

A flight recorder you can replay, and CPU, memory and uncached disk benchmarks.

◐

Native and light

Rust and GPUI, Liquid-Glass styling, light and dark mode, a popout mini monitor and a screensaver.

See it in action

Private by design

No telemetry, no accounts, no network requests. Open Task Manager reads your system with macOS's own tools and keeps its settings on your Mac.
